Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy explains how StudyFlow handles information when you use the website.

1. Study plans stay in your browser

Subjects, topics, completion status, focus lists, and session notes are saved in your browser's local storage. StudyFlow does not currently require an account or send these saved plans to a StudyFlow database. Anyone with access to your unlocked browser profile may be able to access its locally stored plans.

2. Screenshot import and AI processing

When you choose screenshot import, the image files you select are sent to the StudyFlow server and forwarded to Google's Gemini AI service for extraction. The service returns structured text that the website shows for your review. The screenshots are not saved by StudyFlow as a permanent file store, but processing by the third-party provider is subject to that provider's terms and data practices. Avoid uploading sensitive or confidential material.

3. API keys and technical data

The AI service credential is stored server-side and should not be exposed in the website's frontend. The hosting provider and AI provider may process technical information such as request metadata, IP address, usage, and error logs as part of operating their services, subject to their policies.

4. Sharing and selling

StudyFlow does not provide public sharing of your study plans and does not sell your locally stored plan data. If you export a backup, the downloaded file is under your control; store and share it carefully.

5. Retention and deletion

You can remove locally stored data by clearing this site's browser storage. Export a backup first if you need to keep your plans. Requests sent to third-party AI or hosting providers may be subject to their own retention practices.

6. Children and intended use

StudyFlow is a general-purpose study organiser and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not submit another person's private information without permission.

7. Policy updates

This policy may be updated as features change. The latest version will be available at this page.
