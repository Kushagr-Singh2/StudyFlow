MADE FOR STUDENT LIFE

A study planner for your whole semester

SemReady is a study planner for college and university students who want to organise their subjects, keep track of deadlines, and prepare for exams with a clearer plan. Build a semester study plan around your courses and work through it one topic at a time.

Organise subjects and topics

Break each course, project, or skill into phases and manageable tasks, then track your progress as you study.

Turn a syllabus into a checklist

Import screenshots of a study roadmap and review the extracted topics before adding them to your plan.

Plan exam preparation

Set target dates, choose topics for today's focus, and keep your study tasks in one place.

Whether you're planning a new semester or preparing for end-semester exams, SemReady helps you turn a large workload into smaller, actionable steps. Your plans are saved in your browser, and you can export a backup to keep a copy of your work.
